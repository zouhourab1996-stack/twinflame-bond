How does the twin flame calculator work? +
It computes each person's life path number from their full birth date (preserving master numbers 11, 22 and 33), determines both sun signs and their elemental chemistry, then adds both life paths together into a bond number. The final score weights life path compatibility at 40%, zodiac compatibility at 35% and the bond number at 25% — and shows every intermediate result.
What is a good twin flame score? +
88–100 reads as a mirror-grade bond — the territory twin flame stories are written about. 72–87 is a strong twin flame signature, 55–71 is a growth (karmic teacher) bond, and below 55 reads as a catalyst connection: bright and educational rather than mirrored and enduring.
Is my data stored or sent anywhere? +
No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser — nothing is transmitted, saved or logged. Close the tab and it is gone.
What are master numbers in the calculator? +
11, 22 and 33 are the master numbers of numerology — higher-octave paths that are not reduced further. When one or both partners carry a master life path, or when your bond number reduces to a master, the calculator flags it: master energy in a connection is the classic 'old soul, feels fated' signature.
Is numerology scientifically proven? +
No — numerology and astrology are symbolic traditions, not sciences. We built this calculator to be rigorous within those traditions, and we say so plainly. Use the results as mirrors for reflection, not verdicts about a real person.
