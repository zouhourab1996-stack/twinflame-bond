Reading the matrix

How each cell is scored.

Every combination runs through three classical lenses — element chemistry, modality friction, and polarity — plus two special cases: same-sign mirrors and opposite-sign axis pairs.

Which zodiac pairings are the most compatible? +
By element, the strongest pairings are same-element couples (fire-fire, earth-earth, air-air, water-water) and the compatible cross-pairs: fire with air, and earth with water. In twin flame lore specifically, the six opposite-sign 'axis pairs' — Aries-Libra, Taurus-Scorpio, Gemini-Sagittarius, Cancer-Capricorn, Leo-Aquarius, Virgo-Pisces — carry special mirror energy.
What are axis pairs in twin flame astrology? +
Axis pairs are signs sitting exactly opposite each other on the zodiac wheel (six signs apart). They share one life theme from opposite ends — self vs. other, mine vs. ours — which creates the magnetized 'two ends of one axis' feeling that twin flame literature constantly references.
Can incompatible elements still work? +
Yes — friction pairs (fire-water, air-earth, fire-earth, air-water) score lower on natural ease but can produce enormous growth. The matrix gives every pairing a strengths list, watch-outs and a growth line precisely because 'lower score' never means 'impossible' — it means 'requires more translation'.
Is this compatibility matrix based on my whole birth chart? +
No — it works with sun signs, the layer you can compute from birth dates alone. A full synastry reading would include Moon, Venus, Mars and more. Use the matrix as the sun-sign layer of the story, and our twin flame calculator for the numerology layer.
