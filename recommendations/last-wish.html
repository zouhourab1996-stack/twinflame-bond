What this kind of offer does and does not deliver

Every offer on this page belongs to the same genre: you answer a short set of questions, and what comes back is a personalised product — a reading, a drawing, an audio programme, a report. It is worth being clear about what that does and does not establish.

Personalised readings feel accurate for a documented reason, and it is not that they are accurate. In 1948 the psychologist Bertram Forer gave his students a personality test, then handed each of them the identical personality sketch and asked how well it described them. The class rated it, on average, above four out of five. The sketch was assembled from horoscope columns. The effect has been replicated dozens of times since: people accept vague, flattering, individually-addressed descriptions as uniquely true of them, especially when the description contains pairs of opposite traits and the reader believes it was prepared for them specifically.

None of that makes the products worthless. A structured prompt to think about yourself, a daily listening ritual, or a piece of art you like the look of can all be worth what you paid. What it does mean is that the accuracy you feel is not evidence about the world, and the prediction — if the offer makes one — is not supported by anything. Buy it as reflection or as ritual. Do not buy it as information, and do not let it decide anything for you.

What to watch before you pay

  • Check the checkout for a pre-ticked subscription. Several offers in this genre bill monthly after a low entry price, and the cancellation route is not always obvious.
  • Expect upsells. The entry price usually buys the core product, and the "personalised upgrade" is where the margin is.
  • Screenshot the refund terms before you pay. ClickBank's standard window is 60 days and you can claim it through clkbank.com without going through the vendor, but you need your order number.
  • If a reading tells you something about your health, your money or a specific person, treat it as entertainment. That is the line where this stops being harmless.
