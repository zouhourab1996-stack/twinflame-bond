What seeing 7777 does and does not mean

Two things are true at once, and most pages about angel numbers will only give you one of them. The first is that noticing a number and finding meaning in it is a genuine, widespread human experience, and it can be genuinely useful — it focuses attention, and attention is what changes things. The second is that the number itself is not evidence of anything external. Once you start looking for 7777 you will see it, because you now notice every instance of a pattern that was always arriving at roughly the same rate. That is how attention works, not a signal being sent. So: treat it as a prompt to think about a start, not as information about the world. Where people get into trouble is treating it as instruction — making a medical, financial or relationship decision because a number appeared. The number does not know anything you do not.
