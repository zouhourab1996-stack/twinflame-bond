What happens after you write

Everything gets read, and it gets triaged. Corrections go first — if a calculation is wrong, we would rather know today than next month, and we will tell you what we found either way. Feature suggestions go into a running list; some get built, and we will say so if yours does. General questions usually get a reply within a few days.

If you are reporting a wrong result, include the exact inputs — the full date of birth, the name as you entered it, and the number you got versus the number you expected. That is the difference between a bug we can find in ten minutes and one we cannot reproduce at all.

Before you write, check these

  • Date format. Day-month-year versus month-day-year is the single most common cause of a result that looks wrong. Try the other order before assuming the engine is broken.
  • Names. The expression number uses exactly what you type. A shortened name, a married name and a full legal name all produce different figures.
  • Cusp birthdays. If your birthday falls within a day of a sign boundary, sources disagree. That is genuine disagreement, not an error — use a birth-time chart if it matters.
  • Cached pages. If a tool looks broken after an update, a hard refresh usually fixes it.

What we can't help with

We cannot tell you whether a specific person is your twin flame, when or whether you will reunite with someone, or what you should do about a relationship. Not because we are being coy — because nobody can, and the willingness to say so is most of what separates an honest tool from a manipulative one. If someone emails you claiming to be from this site and offering a paid reading, it is not us.
