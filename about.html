How the engines actually work

The tools are not random number generators with spiritual language wrapped around them, and it is worth being able to see the difference. Here is what each one actually computes.

The life path number comes from your date of birth. It is the sum of the day, month and year, each reduced to a single digit first, then added and reduced again — with 11, 22 and 33 preserved as master numbers rather than broken down further. Someone born on 14 March 1995 gets 5 + 3 + (1+9+9+5 = 24 → 6) = 14 → 5. That is arithmetic you can check by hand, which is the point: the calculation is deterministic and you should be able to reproduce it.

The expression number comes from the full birth name, using the Pythagorean system where each letter maps to a digit from 1 to 9 (A, J, S are 1; B, K, T are 2; and so on through to I and R as 9). The letters are summed and reduced the same way. This one depends entirely on the name you enter, so a nickname and a legal name give different results — that is a property of the system, not a bug.

The bond number combines two life paths into a single figure. It is the same reduction applied to the sum, and it is the least meaningful of the three — worth knowing as a conversation starter and not worth treating as a verdict.

The zodiac engine places a date against the tropical sign boundaries. These are the conventional Western dates, which drift very slightly year to year because the equinox does not land at exactly the same moment, so a birthday within a day of a cusp can differ between sources.

The compatibility matrix scores 144 pairings from the interaction of element (fire, earth, air, water) and modality (cardinal, fixed, mutable). Same-element pairs read as instant understanding; fire-air and earth-water as natural supply; fire-water and earth-air as friction. Each page shows the six-dimension breakdown behind the headline number.

What we don't do

Stating this plainly saves everyone time:

  • No predictions. Nothing here forecasts an event, a date, or a reunion. Anyone telling you otherwise about their twin flame tool is selling you something.
  • No paid readings. Every tool on this site is free and runs in your browser. We do not offer personal consultations, and we will never email you offering one.
  • No contact with a specific person. We cannot put you in touch with anyone, and no reading, sketch or report can influence another person's behaviour.
  • No advice on health, money or safety. These tools are reflection, not guidance. If a question has real consequences, it needs a professional rather than a number.
  • No data collection from the tools. The calculations happen on your device. Closing the tab erases everything.

A note on healthy use

Spiritual frameworks can be beautiful scaffolding for growth — and unhealthy fuel for obsession. If a connection or a practice is making you smaller, more anxious, or unwell, please step back and consider talking to a professional. No number, chart or score is a substitute for good mental health care.
